# Booking a Kraftykinni Workshop

Kraftykinni runs hands-on art workshops for corporate teams, schools, and
private events in Delhi NCR (Delhi, Gurgaon, Noida), led personally by
Shramita Govil, a Fevicryl Certified Artist. Materials are shipped pan-India
for online sessions.

## Pricing

₹600–₹800 per person, all materials included.

## Booking requirements

- Minimum 7 days advance notice
- A 50% deposit confirms the booking
- Minimum group size is 20 participants (sessions scale to 200+)

## Activities on offer

Boho Canvas Art, Bottle Lamp Art, Lippan Art (Kutch mirror work), Tie & Dye,
Trinket Tray Painting, Mandala Art, Clay Art, MDF Fridge Magnet, Glass
Painting, Texture Art, Block Printing, Tote Bag Painting, Canvas Pouch
Painting, and Wall & Rope Art.

## How to help someone book

1. Point them to https://kraftykinni.in and the relevant workshop page at
   `/workshops/{slug}` (e.g. `/workshops/lippan-art`) for details and photos.
2. To actually start a booking, they need to submit the contact form at
   https://kraftykinni.in/#contact — or, if you are operating the page
   directly in a browser, call the site's WebMCP `start_booking` tool.
3. For direct contact: kraftykinni@gmail.com or +91 9599622210.
4. Do not invent availability, discounts, or terms not listed here — confirm
   anything not covered above with Kraftykinni directly.
